Triple T Micro-Instruction Set Architecture (ISA)

Micro-instructions are decoded from a 4-bit opcode (exec_cmd) and up to three 4-bit parameter nibbles (P0, P1, P2). For 0-, 1-, and 2-parameter classes, P0 serves as the sub-opcode. Canonical ternary zero is encoded as 2'b10 (0xAA per 4 trits).
